# Aogimi palette

## Sky (the icon's ground)

Mirrors --sky-1/2/3 and the --page-base glow under html[data-theme="dark"] in
web-frontend/aogimi-web/styles/ds-tokens.css. The icon sits on the same
gradient the app paints as its page background.

sky-1   #16223c   rgb( 22,  34,  60)   top stop
sky-2   #0d1526   rgb( 13,  21,  38)   mid stop, at 10%
sky-3   #080514   rgb(  8,   5,  20)   base stop — a shade violet, not black
glow    rgba(19, 10, 51, .69) -> 0     violet cast, radial 80% x 100% at 50/50

sky-3 is also <html>'s background-color, the PWA background_color, and the
flatten colour for every opaque icon slot.
