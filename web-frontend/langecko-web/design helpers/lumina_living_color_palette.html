# Color Palette: Lumina Living Design System This document provides a comprehensive list of the colors used in the **Lumina Living** design system and the associated language learning screens. ### Primary Brand Colors - **Primary Teal:** `#12B5B0` (Main accent color for CTAs, active states, and branding) - **Teal Light:** `#E7F7F6` (Soft background for highlighted elements and badges) - **Teal Dark:** `#0E918E` (Deep teal for high-contrast text or hover states) ### Neutral & Background Colors - **App Background:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50 - The clean, neutral base for the entire interface) - **Sidebar Background:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100 - A subtle shift to define the navigation area) - **Card/Surface Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for elevated content areas like the reader and dictionary) - **Border/Divider:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200 - For soft structural separation) ### Typography & Iconography - **Primary Text:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900 - High legibility for body text and headings) - **Secondary Text:** `#64748B` (Slate 500 - For subtext, labels, and inactive navigation) - **Icon Gray:** `#94A3B8` (Slate 400 - For decorative or secondary icons) ### Feedback & Status Colors (Predicted) - **Success:** `#22C55E` (Green 500) - **Warning:** `#F59E0B` (Amber 500) - **Error:** `#EF4444` (Red 500) --- **Tip:** You can also see these colors in action by clicking the ** View Code** button on any screen to inspect the CSS variables.
